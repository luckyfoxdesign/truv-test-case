Truv is an Open Finance Platform for Lending. Currently Truv partners with lenders, banks, credit unions(CU’s) and helps with income, employment, asset and insurance verification. We also help Banks, FI and CU on Direct deposit switching and more. 

Truv Use cases include but not limited to 

- Mortgage lending, 
- Home Equity & HELOC, 
- Consumer Lending, 
- Auto Lending, 
- Tenant screening and more 

CASE STUDY
